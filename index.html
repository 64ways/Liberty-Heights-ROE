01 / RP

Roleplay Rules

Core standards that protect immersion, fairness, and meaningful scenes.

CORE RULES General roleplay conduct
1.1 Random Killing (RK)

Killing another person with poor reasoning or no proof is considered random killing. Provoking others and following up by killing them, or attempting to do so, is also considered RK or attempted RK.

For when you are allowed to kill someone, read the Gameplay Rule: Escalation Rules.

1.2 Metagaming

Using out-of-character (OOC) information for your own advantage, such as information obtained through Discord or another character, is metagaming and is against the rules.

1.3 Powergaming

Powergaming is performing unrealistic actions for your own benefit, such as forcing actions upon others using /me's or using unrealistic in-game mechanics to gain an advantage.

1.4 Fail RP (FRP)

Fail RP is any unrealistic, low-quality, or non-immersive roleplay action that disrupts a scene and is not covered by another rule, such as metagaming or powergaming.

1.5 Fear RP

Your character should fear for their life as a person would in real life. This includes acting appropriately in high-risk situations and taking them seriously, even while complying with someone's demands.

1.6 New Life Rule (NLR)

If your character is killed (player-killed), you cannot return to the location of your death until 15 minutes have passed or the scene has ended and paramedics, police, and others have left.

You must not remember who killed you or how you died, and this information cannot be used in future interactions—even if other players at the scene tell you what happened.

The only exception is roleplaying your injuries. Refer to Roleplaying Injuries for details.

1.7 Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Continuously running another player over to cause harm is VDM. Running someone over and then killing or attacking them while they are down and out of bounds is also considered VDM.

You may run someone over only if your life is in immediate danger, such as when you are being shot at or aimed at. You must quickly get away and not continue running them over.

A vehicle may be used to run someone over in certain scenes when the intent is not to seriously injure them or repeatedly run them over.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Main Gameplay Rules

Expectations for common interactions and situations across the server.

SERVER RULES Main gameplay
2.4 Robbing Rule

Robberies should be natural, realistic, and not forced in unrealistic scenarios just to earn quick cash.

  • You must have an in-game weapon to rob another player; you cannot attempt a robbery using only your fists.
  • Rob only in areas where a robbery is realistic, with no other witnesses and not in heavily populated areas.
Clarification: a two-minute interaction is not required to rob someone. This note is to clarify the rule following recent reports.
2.5 Spawn Killing

You may not kill anyone who has just spawned into the game. They must be given a chance to reach their apartment or get a vehicle.

2.6 Force Roleplay

Forcing an outcome or scenario without allowing another player to roleplay in response is not permitted. Examples include:

  • Starting an argument without a proper reason and then attacking the other person.
  • Immediately punching someone for being in another gang without escalation.
2.7 Scene Continuity Rule

You may not interrupt or stop a scene by going into /ooc, even if you believe another player is breaking a rule. You may use /ooc to let them know they cannot do something, but it is often unclear who is right in the moment. Record the situation and report it afterward instead.

2.8 ERP (Erotic Roleplay)

Any type of sexual act in-game is prohibited.

2.9 Combat Logging / LTAA

Combat logging is leaving the game to avoid combat or its consequences. LTAA means leaving to avoid arrest.

2.10 Cop Fear

Characters should realistically fear police because of their legal authority, equipment, backup, and ability to arrest or use force. Altercations with police should be a last resort. When committing crimes, consider the realistic consequences of your character's actions.

2.11 Value of Life

Your character must act as though they have one life and naturally fear death or serious injury. Escalated confrontations must not be treated like a game where both sides repeatedly trade attacks without considering the consequences. This includes knife fights and shootouts.

Do not turn fights into continuous back-and-forth attacks. You may respond during a shootout, but once you have been hit, you should stop engaging if the other party is leaving or you have a reasonable opportunity to get away.

03 / FACTIONS

Faction Roleplay — Subset Rules

Standards for set membership, in-character relationships, meetings, and member removals.

GENERAL RULES Subset conduct
G.1 Sanctioning
Everyone repping a set must be sanctioned in-game first.
G.2 Set relations
All set relations must be roleplayed in-game. No sets should claim beef without a valid reason.
G.3 Renegade subset
Every subset must have at least one renegade subset.
G.4 OOC beef
Subsets must not use their servers as a hotspot for out-of-character beef.
G.5 Meetings
All meetings must be roleplayed in-game, not in voice chat.
G.6 RP standards
When gathered, members must showcase their best roleplay skills.
G.7 Rank structure
All sets must have an organized rank structure.
G.8 Beef agreements
Subsets cannot claim beef with other sets without an OOC agreement.
REMOVING MEMBERS Set membership decisions
M.1 Character-specific removal
If a member is kicked from a set, that removal applies only to that specific character.
M.2 OOC issues
You cannot remove someone from your set due to out-of-character issues.
M.3 Consistent FRP
A member who consistently fail-RPs or brings bad reputation to the set may be removed.
M.4 Motherland conflict
A member who is beefing with your set's motherland may be removed.
M.5 Unapproved beef
A member who constantly claims beef with sets your group is not in conflict with may be removed.
M.6 Permission abuse
A member may be removed for abusing permissions.
04 / PROCESS

How To — Beef Drop

Required process for requesting and communicating an approved end to faction beef.

HOW TO Steps to follow
01 Submit a report
If a faction has fail-RPed against you, open a ticket.
02 Receive FM validation
Faction Management (FM) must validate the report for the beef drop to become official.
03 Notify the other owner
Once accepted, DM the owner of the other faction to let them know the beef has been dropped.
DON’TS Do not skip these steps
D.1 No premature drop
Do not drop beef before submitting a report.
D.2 Notify the other owner
Do not drop beef without letting the other faction's owner know.
RULES Approval and consequences
R.1 Valid grounds
You may request to drop beef only if the opposing faction has fail-RPed against you.
R.2 FM approval required
All beef drops must be approved by Faction Management.
R.3 No continuing after a drop
Continuing to beef with the faction after the beef has been officially dropped will result in a strike for Force RP.
05 / CHARACTER

Faction RP — Character Kill Requirements

Evidence quality, authenticity, and character-kill limits.

REQUIREMENTS Clip quality and CK rules
1 Clips must be realistic
  • Do not over-hawk.
  • Do not mag dump.
  • Do not crouch-shoot mid-fight.
  • Do not jump-shoot or jump to avoid being hit.
  • Do not reload mid-fight.
  • Do not shoot from unrealistic distances, such as two blocks away.
2 Clips cannot be staged
Fake or staged videos and planned CKs are not allowed because they serve no roleplay purpose.
3.1 Character CK limit
A character's CK limit is five.
3.2 Forced CKs
Forcing CKs is not allowed and results in a permanent CK blacklist.
3.3 CK permission abuse
Tricking players into CK-ing through the use of CK permissions is not allowed.
3.4 CK hunting
Being CK-hungry results in a one-month CK cooldown.
06 / LAW & FACTIONS

NYPD vs. Faction Rules

Requirements involving Rico situations, hiding, faction membership, rebranding, and character switching.

OCD VS FACTION RULES Active Rico requirements
1.1 Self CKs
You may not stage your own death or CK for the sole purpose of avoiding a RICO.
1.2 Going into hiding
When issued a RICO, your character may roleplay going into hiding. During this time, you cannot switch to another character. This character cannot remain in hiding forever.
1.3 Faction member removals
During an active warrant, faction owners cannot remove members simply to avoid those members being JCK'd or RICO'd. Members may be removed only for extreme rule breaks or in-character switching up, with proof.
1.4 Rebranding
You may not rebrand during active RICOs. You cannot self-disband your faction to avoid a RICO. Doing so will lead to an automatic disband and faction blacklist.
1.5 Character switching
You may not have two characters in the same faction. You cannot switch characters to avoid one of your characters being JCK'd.
1.6 LTAA during RICOs
Leaving to avoid arrest (LTAA) while in the process of being RICO'd will result in an automatic faction disband. You will also receive a 12-day ban for LTAA.
